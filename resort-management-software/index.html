What “resort” means on Transserve

Resort plans bring restaurant-tier features and hotel-tier features together—shared staff and profile tools under one account. Exact modules depend on entitlements.

Restaurant tier

Live orders, POS billing, tables, menus, offers and Captain workflows.

Hotel tier

Rooms, bookings, guest profiles, room QR and room billing / folio.

One tenant

Operate dining and stay without maintaining completely separate product silos.

Plan-based access

Features unlock according to your subscription—availability may vary.

Who it is for

Resorts and hospitality businesses that run both F&B and rooms. Pure restaurants should start with restaurant software; room-only properties with hotel software.
