Restaurant POS Software: Complete Guide for Indian Restaurants

A practical guide to restaurant POS software in India—billing, order tracking, payments, tables and what to compare before you buy.

Indian restaurants usually evaluate POS software for faster billing, clearer order status, and fewer mistakes between the counter, kitchen and floor. This guide explains what matters in daily service—and how Transserve approaches the same problems—without inventing ratings or competitor scores.

What restaurant POS software should cover

  • Quick bill generation for dine-in and takeaway
  • Live order status from new to preparing to ready
  • Table context so the floor knows what is occupied
  • Offers or discounts at billing time
  • Payment recording against each bill
  • Optional waiter/captain mobile workflow

Order-to-payment flow that teams understand

A clear flow reduces shouting across the floor: order placed → preparation tracked → staff serves → bill created → payment recorded. In Transserve, opening a UPI link is not treated as verified payment; the team records payment in the system.

Kitchen prep (often called KOT)

Many Indian restaurants describe kitchen tickets as KOT. What you need operationally is reliable preparation status—not a buzzword. Track orders as they move through cooking so captains and counters stay aligned.

What to compare before you buy

  • Does billing support your dine-in and takeaway mix?
  • Can staff see live order states?
  • Is there a mobile workflow for floor staff?
  • Are prices and plans shown clearly at checkout (not hidden marketing numbers)?
  • Does the vendor avoid claiming unavailable modules (for Transserve: Channel Manager is Coming Soon)?
