Frequently asked questions

Transserve is a complete restaurant and hospitality platform for handling orders, tables, billing and menu management from one place.

It is designed for restaurants, cafés and other small and medium-sized food businesses. Hotel and resort plans are available for hospitality operations.

Use the “Get Started” button to register, then set up your restaurant and menu from the dashboard.

Captains and service staff use it on Android to handle assigned orders and table service.

Use the “Download Captain APK” button in the Captain section when it is available, then follow your device’s installation prompts.

The owner dashboard is a web app and the layout adapts to mobile screens. The Captain app is for Android.

Use the Contact Us link in the footer to email the Transserve team.
