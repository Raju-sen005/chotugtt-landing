## Mapping rules

1. One primary commercial URL per primary keyword cluster.
2. Homepage targets the broad category; deep pages take specific commercial intent.
3. Blog posts capture informational / comparison intent and link up to commercial pages.
4. Hindi pages map 1:1 to English equivalents (hreflang pairs).
5. Avoid cannibalization: POS page owns “restaurant POS”; restaurant management page owns broader RMS.
